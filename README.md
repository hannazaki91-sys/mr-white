# Easy MP3 Downloader for Android

Share or paste a YouTube video or playlist link, or a Spotify song, playlist or
album link, and the app saves the audio as MP3 with title, artist, album and
cover art. Everything runs on the phone. There's no account and no ads.

## Install

1. Download the APK on your phone and open it.
2. When Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to **install unknown apps**,
   then tap **Install**.
3. **Samsung phones:** first turn off **Settings → Security and privacy → Auto
   Blocker**. You can turn it back on after installing.
4. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap **More details →
   Install anyway**. The app isn't on Google Play, because Google Play doesn't
   allow YouTube downloaders.
5. Open the app once with internet on. The first start takes about a minute
   while it prepares its downloader.

**Updating:** install the new APK over the old one. Your settings and downloads
are kept.

## Use

In YouTube or Spotify tap **Share → Easy MP3**, or paste a link, then tap
**Download**. Songs are saved in **Music/EasyMP3Downloader** and appear in your
music player.

Spotify songs are found on YouTube by title, artist and length. A match is a best
guess and is not guaranteed to be the exact recording Spotify plays.

> **Only download audio you own or have permission to download.** The app does
> not get around DRM, paywalls or sign-in requirements.

**طريقة التثبيت:**

1. نزّل الملف على الموبايل وافتحه.
2. لو طلب منك إذن "تثبيت تطبيقات غير معروفة"، وافق ودوس **تثبيت**.
3. **موبايلات سامسونج:** اقفل الأول **الإعدادات ← الأمان والخصوصية ← Auto Blocker**، وتقدر تشغّله تاني بعد التثبيت.
4. لو ظهر تحذير من Google Play Protect، دوس **مزيد من التفاصيل ← التثبيت على أي حال**.
5. افتح التطبيق أول مرة وانت متصل بالإنترنت (التجهيز بياخد حوالي دقيقة).

**الاستخدام:** من يوتيوب أو سبوتيفاي دوس **مشاركة ← Easy MP3**، أو الصق الرابط ودوس **Download**.
الأغاني بتتحفظ في **Music/EasyMP3Downloader**.
